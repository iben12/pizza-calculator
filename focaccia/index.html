Preparation:

  1. Make poolish, let ferment for an hour on room temperature then at least 12 hours in the refrigerator.
  2. Mix ingredients for dough and knead.
  3. Bulk ferment for 50-70 minutes (or until doubled).
  4. Coat deeper baking sheet with olive oil and put the dough in it.
  5. Rest for ca. 10 minutes.
  6. Softly strech the dough towards the baking sheet corners. If it pulls back, let it rest for another 10 minutes.
  7. Drizzle the top with olive oil generously and make holes with your fingers, softly pushing all the way down to the bottom.
  8. Sprinkle with coarse salt and optionally rosemary, dress with whatever you like (tomatoes, prosciutto, etc.).
  9. Let rest for 30-60 minutes.
  10. Preheat oven to 250°C (480°F) and bake for 15-20 minutes.
